Register values are terminal-counted; add 1 for effective values:

$$ L = L_\text{reg} + 1, \qquad S = S_\text{reg} + 1, \qquad L_A = L_{A,\text{reg}} + 1, \qquad L_B = L_{B,\text{reg}} + 1 $$

Leap deltas — per-slot difference from the baseline line length:

$$ \delta_A = L_A - L, \qquad \delta_B = L_B - L $$

Slot $i$ of the 5-slot leap pattern selects $\delta_A$ if bit $i = 0$ and $\delta_B$ if bit $i = 1$:

$$ \delta_i \in \{\delta_A,\, \delta_B\} $$

Leap accumulation over one 5-VSYNC period:

$$ \Delta = \sum_{i=0}^{4} \delta_i $$

Leap-adjusted average line length — $\Delta$ is distributed over $S/2$ lines per VSYNC, averaged across the 5-slot period:

$$ \begin{aligned} L_\text{leap} &= \frac{5LS + 2\Delta}{5S} \\[0.8em] &= L + \frac{2\Delta}{5S} \end{aligned} $$

Horizontal scan frequency:

$$ f_H = \frac{f_\text{VI}}{L_\text{leap}} $$

Vertical scan frequency:

$$ f_V = \frac{2 f_H}{S} = \frac{2 f_\text{VI}}{S \cdot L_\text{leap}} $$

PAL interlaced (1996) example

Given effective values:

$$ \begin{gathered} L = 3178, \qquad S = 625 \\ L_A = 3183\ (\delta_A = 5), \qquad L_B = 3184\ (\delta_B = 6) \end{gathered} $$

The 5-slot leap pattern selects $\delta_A$ if bit $i = 0$ and $\delta_B$ if bit $i = 1$:

$$ \text{pattern} = 10101_2 \implies \delta_B,\, \delta_A,\, \delta_B,\, \delta_A,\, \delta_B $$

Summing leap deltas over one 5-VSYNC period:

$$ \Delta = 6 + 5 + 6 + 5 + 6 = 28 $$

Converting half-lines to full lines:

$$ 2\Delta = 2 \times 28 = 56 $$

Applying the leap-adjusted average line length equation:

$$ \begin{aligned} L_\text{leap} &= \frac{5 \times 3178 \times 625 + 56}{5 \times 625} \\[0.8em] &= \frac{9{,}931{,}250 + 56}{3{,}125} \\[0.8em] &= \frac{9{,}931{,}306}{3{,}125} \end{aligned} $$

Horizontal scan frequency:

$$ \begin{aligned} f_H &= \frac{49{,}656{,}530}{9{,}931{,}306 / 3{,}125} \\[0.8em] &= \frac{49{,}656{,}530 \times 3{,}125}{9{,}931{,}306} \\[0.8em] &= \frac{155{,}176{,}656{,}250}{9{,}931{,}306} \\[0.8em] &= 15{,}625\ \text{Hz} \end{aligned} $$

Vertical scan frequency:

$$ \begin{aligned} f_V &= \frac{2 \times 15{,}625}{625} \\[0.8em] &= \frac{31{,}250}{625} \\[0.8em] &= 50\ \text{Hz} \end{aligned} $$

For modes with no leap correction (NTSC), $\Delta = 0$ and $L_\text{leap} = L$:

$$ \Delta = 0, \qquad L_\text{leap} = L $$
