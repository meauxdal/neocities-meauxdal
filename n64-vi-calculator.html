Register values are terminal-counted; add 1 for effective values:

$$ L = L_\text{reg} + 1, \qquad S = S_\text{reg} + 1 $$

Leap deltas; VI clocks above baseline per slot; bit $i$ of the pattern selects $\delta_A$ (0) or $\delta_B$ (1):

$$ \delta_A = L_A - L, \qquad \delta_B = L_B - L, \qquad \delta_i \in \{\delta_A,\, \delta_B\} $$

Leap accumulation over the 5-VSYNC cycle:

$$ \Delta = \sum_{i=0}^{4} \delta_i $$

Effective average line length:

$$ L_\text{leap} = \frac{5LS + 2\Delta}{5S} $$

Scan frequencies:

$$ f_H = \frac{f_{VI}}{L_\text{leap}}, \qquad f_V = \frac{2f_{VI}}{S \cdot L_\text{leap}} $$
